BEYOND THE OBVIOUS

India's Hidden Gems.

Step away from the crowded tourist trails and discover India's quieter side. From misty valleys to forgotten villages, these places are waiting to be explored.

KERALA

Wayanad

A peaceful escape surrounded by misty hills, dense forests, waterfalls and hidden trails. Wayanad is perfect for travellers looking for nature, adventure and quiet moments.

BEST FOR Nature & Adventure
BEST TIME October – May
NORTHEAST

Sikkim

Discover peaceful mountain villages, winding roads, colourful monasteries and dramatic Himalayan landscapes away from the usual tourist routes.

BEST FOR Mountains & Culture
BEST TIME March – June
ARUNACHAL PRADESH

Ziro Valley

A peaceful valley surrounded by green hills, pine forests and charming villages. Ziro is known for its slow pace, beautiful landscapes and unique local culture.

BEST FOR Nature & Culture
BEST TIME March – October
KASHMIR

Gurez Valley

A remote Himalayan valley surrounded by dramatic mountains, rivers and traditional wooden villages. Gurez offers a quieter side of Kashmir.

BEST FOR Mountains & Photography
BEST TIME May – September
ASSAM

Majuli

Set along the Brahmaputra, Majuli is known for its island landscapes, traditional villages, art, culture and peaceful riverside atmosphere.

BEST FOR Culture & Slow Travel
BEST TIME October – March
UTTARAKHAND

Chopta

Surrounded by alpine meadows and Himalayan peaks, Chopta is a beautiful base for peaceful mountain walks, trekking and sunrise views.

BEST FOR Trekking & Mountains
BEST TIME March – June
NAGALAND

Dzukou Valley

Rolling green landscapes, seasonal flowers and dramatic mountain scenery make Dzukou Valley one of Northeast India's memorable trekking destinations.

BEST FOR Trekking & Adventure
BEST TIME June – September
MAHARASHTRA

Tarkarli

A coastal escape known for clear waters, quiet beaches, traditional villages and a relaxed Konkan atmosphere away from busy beach towns.

BEST FOR Beaches & Relaxation
BEST TIME October – March
